Home MCP (Ian Cleary)

Personal smart-home integration

Home MCP (Ian Cleary) is a personal, single-user integration that connects Ian Cleary's own Google Home household to his own personal AI assistant.

It is not a commercial product. It is not offered to anyone else, there is no sign-up, and it cannot be used by anyone other than Ian.

To work, it asks Google for permission to read Ian's Google Home device state and event history (the home.platform.v2 scope) — only for his own household, and only so his assistant can answer his questions and carry out actions he approves.

How that data is handled is described in the privacy policy.
