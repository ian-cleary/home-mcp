Privacy Policy — Home MCP (Ian Cleary)

Dated 16 October 2026

This privacy policy covers Home MCP (Ian Cleary), a personal, single-user integration that connects Ian Cleary's own Google Home household to his own personal AI assistant. It is not a commercial product and is not available to anyone else.

  • What is accessed. The integration requests access to Google Home device state and event history, via the home.platform.v2 scope, for Ian's own household only. It accesses no other accounts and no other data.
  • Why. The data is used solely so Ian's personal assistant can answer his questions about his home (for example, device states and recent activity) and carry out actions he approves.
  • No sharing. The data is not sold, not shared with any third party, and not used for advertising.
  • No unnecessary retention. Data is not kept beyond what is needed to operate the integration. Cached access tokens are stored locally and are refreshed or deleted as part of normal operation.
  • Revoking access. Access can be revoked at any time from Ian's Google Account permissions page, and fully removed by deleting the associated Google Cloud project.

Contact. Questions about this integration: ian.cleary@gmail.com.
